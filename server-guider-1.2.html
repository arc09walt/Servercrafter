SERVER BUILDER GUIDER

A walk-through that stands up a small home server on a plain Windows PC — with your hands. It prepares everything, points at the exact press, shows you what right looks like, and proves each step before moving on. It never runs the server. It never presses anything. Three acts: light the house, walk the sidewalk, keep the street door.
